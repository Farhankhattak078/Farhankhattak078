### 🔥 Featured Projects

**[💬 AI E-Commerce Assistant (GenAI + RAG)](https://github.com/Farhankhattak078/ai-ecommerce-assistant)**
GenAI-powered e-commerce chatbot using **LLaMA 3.3 via GROQ**. Classifies user intent (FAQ vs. product/SQL queries) and answers with real-time database lookups — natural language in, filtered product results or policy answers out. Streamlit front end, includes a web-scraping pipeline for product data.

**[🚗 Vehicle Damage Detection (Deep Learning)](https://github.com/Farhankhattak078/Vehicle-Damage-Detection)**
Deep learning system classifying vehicle damage into 6 categories (front/rear × breakage/crushed/normal). Built and benchmarked 4 architectures in **PyTorch** — custom CNN, regularized CNN, ResNet50, and EfficientNet-B0 — with ResNet50 winning at **79% accuracy**. Deployed via Streamlit.

**[🛡️ Shield Insurance — Premium Prediction & Risk Analysis](https://github.com/Farhankhattak078/Shield-Insurance-Premium-Prediction-Risk-Analysis)**
Full ML pipeline for predicting insurance premiums — hit **~99% R² with XGBoost**, beating Linear Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest baselines. Engineered a custom "Lifestyle Risk Score" that ranked as a top feature.

**[🏥 Healthcare Premium Prediction](https://github.com/Farhankhattak078/HealthCare_Premium_Predication)**
Predicts health insurance premiums using **age-segmented ML models** for better accuracy across life stages. Full pipeline from EDA to deployment, served through an interactive Streamlit app.

**[🏠 House Price Prediction & Analysis](https://github.com/Farhankhattak078/House-Price-Prediction-Analysis)**
End-to-end pipeline on housing data — improved model performance by 12% through feature engineering and hyperparameter tuning (GridSearchCV, RandomizedSearchCV).
